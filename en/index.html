Founder

Waleed Mamdouh

Founder of Waleed Modern Investment Sultanate of Oman

Waleed Mamdouh founded Waleed Modern Investment in Salalah in 2020 with a clear goal: every shop and canteen in Oman should get trusted food products, at a fair wholesale price, on time.

Under his leadership the company became the exclusive agent for Matrix and Sun Ice in the Sultanate, launched its own Oman-made brand Sun Crunch, and saw its products added to the Ministry of Education supply order in every governorate.

Waleed believes trade is a long relationship before it is a deal — which is why he stays in direct contact with customers and holds every order to the same standard of quality and punctuality.
